# Bestiary: draft descriptions (for you to rewrite)

Written 2026-11-05 as a first pass, in the voice of a field note by Alaron: one to three short sentences each, shown next to his sketch.
Every fact in them is something the game itself says about the creature (its attacks, spells and resistances from the ROM's creature table,
its place in the story from the game and the plot summaries) or a plain description of what it is. Where a line leans on something I am not sure
of, it ends with **[?]**. Nothing is copied from the StrategyWiki table: the numbers the bestiary shows in the game come from the game's own tables.

Sources read: the game's ROM (the creature table: levels, stats, weapons, spells, resistances), [StrategyWiki: Enemies](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Aidyn_Chronicles:_The_First_Mage/Enemies)
(table by GlacialBear and LoneSoldier: loot and chapter hints, no lore), [StrategyWiki: Characters](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Aidyn_Chronicles:_The_First_Mage/Characters)
(the party's manual text) and the [Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aidyn_Chronicles:_The_First_Mage) (the plot). There is **no lore text for any enemy** in the ROM, the wiki or the plot summaries:
the descriptions are mine, from the data, so please correct anything that doesn't match your play.

**One thing the game gets wrong:** a creature's resistance is a number its damage is multiplied by (0 immune, 0.5 half, 1 normal, 1.5 or 2 more),
but Sense Aura calls anything between 0 and 2 "Resistant" and only 2 and over "Vulnerable". The StrategyWiki's "Resistant: Fire" for the Troll and Tomb Rat, "Resistant: Naming" for the Hobgoblin
and Kitarak, "Resistant: Lunar" for the Dracovern, and so on, are really **weaknesses** (they take half again as much). The bestiary can show the true number
("takes 150% from Fire"); these descriptions follow the numbers.

How the entries are grouped (a creature appears once, under its name, however many level variants the game has: "Goblin" is levels 1 to 6).

## Goblins

- **Goblin**: The small raiders whose poison started all this. They fight with sabre or broadsword, and the bigger ones carry bows. Slow to learn fear. [? "small": check the models]
- **Goblin Scout**: A goblin sent ahead, lightly armoured and quick, who prefers to shoot from a distance.
- **Goblin Sergeant**: Keeps a goblin band together and fights better than the rank and file. Also keeps his distance and shoots when he can.
- **Goblin Poisoner**: Throws poisoned darts. Keep your healer close.
- **Hobgoblin**: Larger and tougher than a goblin, in heavy armour, with a spirit shield to cast and a trick for draining stamina. Naming magic hurts it more than it should.
- **Goblin Assassin**: A goblin who kills for a living. Very quick, with a sabre and poisoned darts. Strike first, or not at all.
- **Goblin Lieutenant**: Leads a goblin band, with a longsword and throwing hatchets. Tougher than the rank and file, and knows it.
- **Goblin Raider**: A goblin who raids: light on his feet, with a sabre and a great bow. Hard to pin down.
- **Goblin Warrior**: A goblin who fights at the front, with a broadsword and a strong back. More of a fighter than a thief.
- **Goblin Archer**: Keeps his distance behind a long bow, with a broadsword for when you come close.
- **Goblin Commander**: Commands a band: stout, with a broadsword and throwing axes. Fights harder than he talks.
- **Goblin Robber**: A thief with a hunter's bow and a longsword. Takes what he can, and runs when he can't.
- **Hobgoblin Major**: A hobgoblin of rank, with a greataxe and a few spells: clumsiness, light, and webs and shields of starlight. Naming magic hurts it more than it should.

## Bandits

- **Human Bandit**: A road thief with a sabre and a shield. Not much of a fighter, but they come in numbers.
- **Bandit Woodsman**: A bandit who shoots. Stay out of the open.
- **Bandit Boss**: Leads a gang. Better armed than his men, with a bow of his own, and some of them know tricks that tire you out.
- **Bandit Archer**: A bandit who shoots, and shoots well. Keep moving.
- **Bandit Chieftain**: Leads a gang in heavy armour, with a great bow. Knows tricks that bolster him and addle you.
- **Bandit Namer**: A bandit who has learned Naming magic: charms, and spells that sharpen his wits and dull yours. Fights with a mace and a bow.
- **Bandit Necromancer**: A bandit who has turned to the dead arts: darkness, haste, walls of bones, and a spell that drains your vigour.
- **Bandit Raider**: A bandit who rides with the raiders. A mace, a long reach, and not easy to wear down.
- **Human Renegade**: A man gone bad, with a stealthy blade and darts thrown from range. Quick, and not easily cornered.
- **Human Rogue**: A thief with a morningstar and javelins. Throws before he strikes.
- **Bandit Brute**: Built for the front: a longsword, thrown stars, and a great many hit points.
- **Bandit Goon**: The lowest of the gang: a blade and not much else. They come in numbers.
- **Bandit Ranger**: Shoots from cover with a long bow, and draws a bandit blade when pressed.
- **Bandit Thug**: A heavy bandit with a broadsword, who throws burning bottles when you keep your distance.
- **Bandit Elementalist**: A bandit who has learned the elements: wind, dragon flames, an air shield, and enough might to swing a sword of it.
- **Bandit Star Mage**: A bandit who has learned star magic: lights, shields of starlight, clumsiness, and Frozen Doom.
- **Shadow Bandit**: One of the Shadow gang. Casts darkness and a spirit shield, and drains your stamina. Shoots, too.
- **Shadow Chieftain**: Leads the Shadow gang. Hastes himself, drains stamina, shields himself, and shoots a great bow.

## Beasts

- **Giant Rat**: Larger than any rat has a right to be. Gnaws, and is not hard to put down. Its hide is worth something.
- **Giant Bat**: Hunts in the dark. Sunlight hurts it more than steel does.
- **Darkenbat**: A bigger, nastier bat. Shrugs off little, except daylight.
- **Tomb Rat**: Lives among old graves. Fire hurts it worse than most things.
- **Wolf**: A hunting animal that attacks in a pack. Moonlight makes it stronger.
- **Dire Wolf**: A wolf grown huge. Strong in moonlight like the rest of them.
- **Spirit Wolf**: Not flesh at all: ordinary weapons pass through it. Spells that darken and exhaust.
- **Boar**: Charges and gores. Tough hide, little cunning.
- **Giant Boar**: A boar the size of a cart. Hits like one.
- **Bear**: Strong, and not afraid of a party. Best met from a distance.
- **Cave Bear**: The bigger, angrier bear of the caves. Plenty of hit points and no hurry.
- **Large Scorpion**: Hard shell, quick sting. Its plates can be salvaged.
- **Giant Scorpion**: A larger one, harder to hurt.
- **Giant Squid**: Seen in the water. Fire hurts it more than most creatures. [?: where it is met]
- **Sand Worm**: Rises out of the desert sand. Takes fire and earth magic poorly.
- **Salamander**: A fire-living lizard that cannot be burned at all.
- **Lava Hound**: A dog of living fire: immune to flame, and water is its weakness. Casts Immolation.
- **Hellhound**: Bites, and breathes dragonfire. Fire does nothing to it, and ordinary blows only little.
- **Wyvern**: A small, spiteful dragon: mirrors, webs of starlight, and a sting. Wind hurts it badly; magic does less than it should.
- **Dracovern**: A winged beast with a long sting. Moon magic hurts it more than it should.
- **Gryphon**: Half eagle, half lion. Strong and not shy of a fight.
- **Manticore**: Seen shooting at a distance. A lion's body, and worse. [?: check what it shoots]
- **Harpy**: A winged woman-thing that saps stamina. Do not let it tire you.
- **Gorgon**: Casts Frozen Doom. Ordinary weapons do half. Keep your distance. [?: stone-turning is not in the data]
- **Cyclops**: One eye and a long reach. Throws things from a distance. Moon magic does less to it than it should.
- **Minotaur**: Bull-headed and bad-tempered. A brute with an axe.
- **Minotaur Lord**: A great one, with more hit points than anything else of its kind. Magic dulls its strength a little.
- **Ogre**: Big, slow, and swings a giant axe. Hits hard enough that you should not stand in front of it.
- **Ogre Boss**: Leads other ogres. Larger, and sometimes throws things from afar.
- **Troll**: Takes a great many hits, and fire hurts it more than blades do.
- **Ogre Guard**: An ogre set to watch over something. Swings a giant axe, and knows a little against poison.
- **Cerberus**: A three-headed hound out of the underworld. Breathes dragonfire, and throws fireballs when it cannot bite.
- **Dire Bear**: A bear grown bigger and meaner. Slow to fall, and hits like a falling door.
- **Plague Rat**: A rat that carries sickness. Quick, and its bite is the worst of it.
- **Scorpion**: A scorpion of the sands, in a hard shell with a quick sting.

## Lizard Men

- **Lizard Man**: Armoured in scales with a turtleshell shield and a poleaxe. At home in water, and takes water magic poorly.
- **Lizard Man Sgt**: Drills the others. Throws javelins when you keep your distance.
- **Lizard Man Boss**: Their chieftain. [? little data: check it in play]
- **Lizard Man King**: The lizard-men's king. Swings a great axe, casts dragon flames and fireballs, and is not to be met lightly.

## The restless dead

- **Skeleton**: Blades barely mark it; a smashing weapon does far better.
- **Skeleton Archer**: Shoots from a distance, with a short sword in reserve. Blades barely mark it; a smashing weapon does well.
- **Giant Skeleton**: A very large skeleton wielding a maul. Blades barely mark it; a smashing weapon does well.
- **Zombie**: Slow, strong, and not easily harmed by blows or magic.
- **Plague Zombie**: A diseased zombie with far more hit points. Even harder to put down.
- **Wight**: A grave thing that casts frost and white fire. Ordinary blows pass through it, and magic does only half.
- **Wraith**: Ordinary blows pass through it. It casts darkness, and it can touch you with death.
- **Shadow**: A shapeless thing of darkness that shields against whole schools of magic. Neither blows nor spells harm it.
- **Shamsuk**: The necromancer in the bog, who cursed Niesen and destroyed Pome. Casts haste, weakness and acid, and raises walls of bones.
- **Lugash**: A dark caster who slows the clumsy and walls them in with bones. [?: who he is]
- **Skeleton Warrior**: A skeleton with a blade and the will to use it, who raises walls of bones. Blades barely mark it; a smashing weapon does far better.

## Elementals and constructs

- **Air Elemental**: A whirlwind with a will. Neither blows nor wind harm it.
- **Dust Devil**: A small windstorm that debilitates and strikes with lightning. Immune to air.
- **Earth Elemental**: Stone given purpose. Immune to earth and to ordinary blows.
- **Fire Elemental**: A living flame, immune to ordinary blows and to fire. Casts Immolation.
- **Water Elemental**: A body of water on its feet. Ordinary blows and water pass through it.
- **Stone Golem**: A made thing, hard to hurt with blows or magic.
- **Giant Golem**: A far larger one. Slow, with no weakness I know of.

## Chaos

- **Chaos Scout**: The lightest of the Chaos soldiers, quick and with a bow. Can cast haste on itself.
- **Chaos Warrior**: A heavy fighter in chain. Carries a Chaos shield.
- **Chaos Trooper**: Swings a blood axe and smites the ground with earth magic.
- **Chaos Stormer**: Calls down lightning, and carries a maul for when it runs short.
- **Chaos Sorceror**: Shields itself with starlight and mirrors, and throws lightning. Resists magic.
- **Chaos Spellweaver**: Weakens you, clumsies you, and raises walls of bones. Resists magic.
- **Chaos Mauler**: A giant with a maul that fights spells of elemental or dark kind, and of Frozen Doom. Hard to hurt with blows.
- **Chaos Slayer**: A frontline killer who hastes itself, shields with starlight and weakens you. Resists physical blows.
- **Chaos Lieutenant**: Commands the Chaos soldiers. Hard to hurt with blows or magic, and walls you in with bones.
- **Chaos Major**: One of the greater Chaos captains. Fights with fireballs and the stars' gravity. Resists the sun.
- **Marquis**: A Chaos lord. Casts haste and clumsiness, and wears an amulet. Hard to hurt. [?]
- **Pochanargat**: The leader of Chaos, the monster in my first vision, in Oriana's hut. Casts mirrors, fireballs and Frozen Doom.
- **Sheridan**: The prince of Gwernia, who was jealous of me.
