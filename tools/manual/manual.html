Getting Started

Aidyn ReChronicled is Aidyn Chronicles: The First Mage (Nintendo 64, 2001) rebuilt as a native PC program. The game's own code was translated into C, so it plays exactly as it did on the console. On top of that it runs at high resolution, in widescreen and at high frame rates, with redrawn menus and textures.

What you need

  • Your own dump of the North American 1.0 cartridge (.z64). The port contains no game data, and it checks the dump before it will run it.
  • Optionally, the same dump with the community Aidyn Patch or Aidyn Plus applied (each player applies them to their own dump; the port checks the result).
  • A graphics card with Vulkan, and an Xbox-style controller (the keyboard works too).

The first start

Start the game once with your dump:

AidynReChronicled --rom /path/to/aidyn-chronicles.z64

The dump is checked, copied into the game's data folder and remembered, so after that the game starts without it. It goes straight to the title screen. Press ≡ (Menu) or Enter to begin.

F1 opens this manual in your browser at any time (the game leaves fullscreen so you can see it). F2 turns the newly painted portraits, icons and textures off and on again, to compare. F11 or Alt+Enter switches fullscreen on and off, and Shift+Esc quits. The app launchers have a Manual entry too.

Everything the game keeps lives in ~/.local/share/AidynReChronicled: your saves (aidyn.mpk), settings.json, the stored dumps, the HD texture packs and this manual.

Playing the Game

Choose New Game on the main menu and name the hero. His name is Alaron unless you change it: press ≡ to keep it, or pick letters with the stick or D-pad and A (B deletes a letter). Choose OK to accept the name, or the X to go back to the main menu unchanged.

Saving and Loading

Your progress is kept on a Controller Pak, the N64's memory card. Save from the pause menu whenever you like; load from the main menu or the pause menu.

Save Game

  • Save: choose a save with the stick left and right and press A. Saving over an old game replaces it.
  • Delete: choose a save and press A to remove it from the pak.
  • Exit: back to the pause menu.

Load Game

  • Load: choose a save with the stick left and right and press A.
  • Delete and Exit work as above.

The Controller Pak is a file, aidyn.mpk in the data folder (aidyn-patch.mpk for Aidyn Patch). It is written the moment the game saves. It is the same kind of file N64 emulators use, so a pak can be copied in or out.

The game keeps copies of it in backups/ in the data folder: one each time the game starts and one before each save, the newest 20. If a save goes wrong, or Alaron falls, copy one back over aidyn.mpk while the game is closed.

The Display

The game has two modes. Trek Mode is travel: walking between towns, exploring, talking. When enemies attack you switch to Combat Mode.

Alaron
Alaron stands for the whole party while it travels.
Sun dial
The sun and moon circle a twelve-hour dial as the day passes. The roads are dangerous after dark.
Compass
The direction you face is lit; the others are shaded.
The party
Press B to show everyone's portraits along the bottom.

In widescreen the sun dial moves to the left edge and the compass to the right edge, leaving the middle of the picture clear. The party portraits stay in the middle.

Dialogue

Walk up to a friendly character and press A to talk. The two of you take turns until the conversation is over. When Alaron has several things he could say, pick one with the stick and press A. Once a topic is finished new questions may appear, but there is no going back to an earlier list, so choose with care.

Buying and Selling

Talk to a merchant with A to buy, sell, train or leave. Before buying, press Y to see whether the price is fair. On the trading page, move the stick left and right to switch between Buy and Sell, and up and down to choose an item. Your gold is shown at the top. Under Buy you see what an item costs; under Sell, what you would be paid for it.

Not every merchant trains. See After Combat for how characters improve.

Combat

When enemies attack, the whole party appears on a battlefield facing them. Fights are turn-based: each character moves and acts in turn, and how far they can move depends on their stats. A heavily armoured knight is slow but hits hard face to face. A thief darts around a slower enemy to strike from behind. A spellcaster with many spells can catch enemies in the open. High ground gives a bonus, and a bow strikes from a distance.

Turn-Based Attacks

On a character's turn, move them with the stick and press A to attack. Spellcasters can cast with X instead. A character who can't attack ends the turn with LT, LB or A.

The Battlefield

The character in play
Your party is lined up along one side of the screen; the character whose turn it is stands in the middle.
Enemies
Each enemy in the fight is shown along the other side.
Health bars
Next to each portrait, a bar shows hit points. It shrinks and changes colour as the character is hurt; when it is gone the character falls. Enemies have bars too.
Messages
The bottom of the screen says whose turn it is and whether an attack hit or missed.
Spells and items
Hold X (spells) or Y (items) and move the stick up and down to choose; let go and press A to use it.

After Combat

Level Up

After a victory the Level Up page lets you spend experience on stats, skills and spells: choose one and press A. Move up and down to see the XP Cost of each next step; it can't be bought when the cost is higher than your XP Remaining. More victories bring more experience. ≡ or B closes the page. See Player Stats.

Treasure

Next comes the treasure the enemies dropped. Highlight an item and press A to take it; you can also drop things you carry to make room. The gold you won is shown at the bottom. ≡ or B returns to the journey.

Magic

A character who knows spells can cast them in battle. On their turn, hold X to list their spells, choose one with the stick and let go. Then press A to start casting. Every spell uses up stamina, and most also use up an ingredient: spice , herb or gemstone .

Attack Spells

Choose a damaging spell, steer the cursor onto the enemy you want with the stick and press A.

Spells for the Party

To heal, protect or strengthen, choose the spell and, if it is for one character, highlight them and press A. A spell for several characters shows a large circle instead: everyone inside it is affected.

Schools and Aspects

Every spell belongs to a school: Elemental , Naming , Necromancy , Star or neutral. Most are also Solar or Lunar . Characters have an aspect too. An aspected spell is harder to resist when the caster's aspect differs from the target's, and easier when they are the same.

Learning a spell, and raising its rank, costs experience and needs enough skill in Wizard. Every spell in the game is in the Spell List.

Pausing the Game

Press ≡ at any time to pause. B or ≡ returns to the game. RB turns the scroll to the next page: the inventory, then the calendar. LB or LT turns it back.

Camping

Camping lets the party rest and win back hit points and anything else that recovers with time. Pause with ≡, turn to the calendar page with RB, choose Camp and press A.

Monsters can find a camp at night. A party member with a high Ranger skill makes an ambush much less likely.

Player Stats

Characters learn from every battle. Each defeated enemy brings experience, which buys higher stats and skills (on the Level Up page after a fight). There are six stats. Skills are learned and trained with masters and teachers in towns and villages.

Intelligence
Strengthens a mage's spells and a warrior's ranged attacks.
Willpower
Resistance to enemy magic.
Dexterity
Quickness: extra attacks, a better chance to dodge, and more ground covered in battle.
Endurance
Wounds that can kill come off Endurance: when it reaches zero, death is close. Raising it also adds to a character's hit points.
Strength
Harder blows, and a better chance to hit.
Stamina
Used up by casting spells, disarming traps, picking locks and making armour and potions. If it runs out, further strain comes off Endurance, so rest to keep it up.

Skills

Alchemist Diplomat Healer
Loremaster Mechanic Merchant
Ranger Stealth Thief
Troubadour Warrior Wizard

A few to remember: Ranger keeps camps safe, Thief opens locks and disarms traps, and Wizard decides which spells a character can learn.

Weapons & Items

Alaron will find things to use, wear and sell everywhere he goes.

Weapons
Swords, hafted weapons, pole arms, thrown weapons and missile weapons, from shops or from the field after a battle. A fast weapon can beat a heavy one, because quick attacks hit more often. Give each character weapons that suit their skills.
Armour
Protection against enemy blows. Equip the best you can for everyone, but heavy armour slows quick characters down.
Rings
Change a character's protection, spellcasting or fighting ability.
Potions
Healing and curing potions save lives deep in the forest. Remember them in combat.
Scrolls
Spells written down. Equip a scroll and its spell can be cast in battle with X; use it from the inventory page to learn the spell for good.

Treasure Chests

Beaten enemies drop items, and chests are hidden all over the realm, usually off the beaten path. They hold anything from gold to armour and weapons. Press A to open one. If it is trapped you are asked whether to disarm the trap; fail, and the whole party is hurt when the chest opens. A locked chest needs a party member to pick the lock. Without enough Thief skill, a chest stays shut.

Send the party member with the best Thief skill to open chests.

Characters

Alaron

A young squire with a quick temper, orphaned as a baby and raised at the King's court. He is training to be a knight, if he can keep out of trouble. He has no idea that his life is about to change for good.

Brenna

Orphaned young, Brenna looked after herself until the King took her in. She is a gifted thief (never say so to her face) and Alaron's oldest friend, though the two argue all the time.

Abrecan

Captain of the King's guard and a knight through and through, sworn to honour and duty. Under the gruff manner he worries that Alaron will never live up to his promise. Nobody handles a weapon better.

Rheda

One of the youngest wizards in the kingdom and Alaron's teacher in magic. Her passion runs away with her at times; above all she wants to cure her friend Niesen of a necromancer's curse.

Godric

The castle alchemist: brilliant, scatterbrained, and dangerous to stand next to while he works. Nobody knows more about herbs and potions.

Becan

A knight who once saved the King's life in battle and then turned his back on war, to live alone as a tracker and woodsman. He wants to help Alaron, but going back to the fighting troubles him.

Arturo

A walking fortress in armour with not much going on inside. All he wants is to be a knight, and he will do anything to become one. A loyal friend, even if his idea of tactics needs work.

Keelin

A beautiful thief who gets people what they want, for a price. Now she wants something for herself: Alaron. Useful, and sometimes more trouble than she is worth.

Niesen

Rheda's friend from the Wizards' School in Talewok, cursed by a necromancer to turn slowly undead. He joins Alaron looking for a cure, and his fate turns out to be bound up with Alaron's own.

Donovan

A swaggering duelist who has never heard of surrender or caution. His sword arm may be as good as he says, but not every danger ahead can be fought with a blade.

Baird

Half poet, half barbarian, and the hero of his own epic, at least in his own mind. He bursts into verse at the worst moments, but he is very good to have around in a fight.

Dougal

A travelling troubadour who fights and casts spells as well as he sings. Friendly to everyone, and strangely quiet about where he comes from.

Sholeh

One of the Jundar, a mysterious people who look down on humans, though Sholeh finds them oddly interesting. A Star mage and seasoned warrior whose loyalty is hard to read.

Aidyn Patch

Aidyn Patch is a community patch for the 1.0 cartridge by u/halibabica (r/Aidyn). It rebalances the game: potion recipes and prices, how fast skills and weapons gain experience, and much of the item, spell and enemy data. It changes data only, not the game's code, so the PC version runs it as it is.

Installing and switching

  • Apply the patch to your own 1.0 dump and start the game once with --rom and the patched file. It is checked and kept beside the original. The same goes for Aidyn Plus, a bigger rebalance (new weapon skills and items, every spell learnable, elements reworked; its own FAQ explains it).
  • On the title screen the bottom left lists Original, Aidyn Patch and Aidyn Plus, the one you are playing lit. X and Y (K, I) switch to the other two, as marked: the game restarts in that version and remembers the choice. One you haven't added is grey and marked not found.
  • --original, --patch or --plus on the command line picks a version for one run. The launchers "Aidyn ReChronicled", "(Aidyn Patch)" and "(Aidyn Plus)" do the same.
  • Each version has its own Controller Pak (aidyn.mpk, aidyn-patch.mpk, aidyn-plus.mpk). Copy one over another to carry a save across.

ReChronicled

What is new in this edition, compared with playing the cartridge:

Sharp at any resolution
The game is drawn at 1080 lines or more (supersampled for smoother edges) with 4x anti-aliasing, instead of the N64's 240.
Widescreen
16:9, 21:9 or any width. The 3D view widens, and the HUD moves to the screen edges, walking and in combat. The pause menu and shops stay 4:3, the shape they were made for.
Smooth motion
The game itself runs at 60 frames a second (the N64 managed 15 to 20). Above that, frames are drawn in between, up to 120 by default. Walking and running no longer stop for a moment every stride.
HD menus and textures
Menus, pictures, icons and portraits are upscaled from the game's own art; the text is redrawn in a clean font, and the button prompts show Xbox buttons. The world's textures are upscaled too.
Further draw distance
Outdoors, more of the land around you is loaded and drawn, and trees and other objects show from further away.
A free camera
The right stick turns the camera around the party and tilts it, both at the same speed; either direction can be inverted.
Fast-forward
Click the left stick (or hold Tab) and everything runs three times faster: long walks, fights, scenes. Two chevrons at the top right show it is on. Enemy turns in combat run twice as fast on their own.
Save backups
The last 20 states of your Controller Pak are kept (see Saving and Loading).
Straight to the title
The start-up logos are skipped.
Aidyn Patch and Aidyn Plus on the title screen
See Aidyn Patch.
