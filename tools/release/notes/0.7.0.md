## What's new since 0.6.0

- **The HD menu and world packs are in the download**: the game's pictures that aren't repainted yet (menu pieces,
  effects, a few portraits and titles) come upscaled, as on the developer's own machine. Only what the painted art
  doesn't replace is included, so the download stays small.
- **Restored music**: the music's instrument samples are redrawn whole, their lost treble back and the 8-bit hiss
  gone, the same notes and mix (Instruments on the ReChronicled page: Restored or Original). Sound: Enhanced plays
  the game as loud as other games and a little clearer.
- **Quit to Title and Quit Game**: the Menu page ends with Quit to Title (it asks first: anything not saved is lost),
  in a fight too; the title screen's menu ends with Quit Game.
- **Load Game and Quick Load in fights**: both are on a fight's Menu page now; the fight is cleared away and the save
  loads.
- **Combat speed and Enemy turns** on the ReChronicled page: your party's moves and attacks 1.5x, 2x or 3x (never while
  you choose), the enemies' turns up to 4x.
- **HP and stamina inside the portraits' frames** in combat, for the party and the enemies, instead of bars hanging off
  the side.
- **Crowded fights**: attackers no longer stall against each other; they slide past and take a free spot by their
  target (a pack of wolves now actually attacks).
- **The character page** shows the three parts of the hit points beside Level and Gold: END, STA and VIG (Endurance,
  Stamina and the hidden vigour), and the HitPoints rope is repainted.
- **LT switches characters** in the pause menu, shops and with trainers (with RT), and View no longer does.
- **Anti-aliasing on the Display page**: Off, Low, Medium or High (MSAA and supersampling together, applied at once).
- **Combat descriptions** also for the X menu's skills (Lay On Hands, Heal With Herbs, Perform), with what a spell,
  skill or potion takes from the pack and its icon.
- **Fixes**: the red target box and the portrait's glow no longer stay after a cancelled spell; small text (the combat
  log, the descriptions) is evenly spaced without cut letters.
- **An icon of its own for every item**: 175 items that shared 31 icons (every shield one shield, every ring one ring,
  every map one parchment) each have their own painted one now.
- **Potions told apart**: every potion has its own colour (Fire Flask orange, Sleep Gas lavender, Antidote green,
  Stealth dusky violet...; Healing keeps its red), in the inventory, the shops and the combat item menu. Scrolls and
  wands take the colour of their spell's school (Chaos red, Elemental amber, Naming green, Necromancy violet, Star
  blue).
- **The last world textures redrawn**: the glows, smoke and spell effects that were still upscales (69 of them) are
  now redrawn smoothly from the originals, so 2,113 of the game's 2,115 world textures are hand-made.
- **The world map repainted**: the map page's land, revealed region by region as you find each map, is newly painted
  in the page's sepia ink: forests, hills, towns and roads.
- **What a spell or potion does, in combat**: while a spell is highlighted in the held X menu, or a potion or an
  item's spell in the held Y menu, its description shows beside the menu (above it in 4:3), the same text as the
  pause menu's Details. It hides with the combat log.
- **Characters at the edges of a widescreen picture**: people, creatures and props near the sides of a wide screen
  were left undrawn (the game only drew what a 4:3 picture showed). They now show right to the edge.
- **More button hints**: Open on the map page, Scroll and Close in the journal, Leave in the shops, and Use on
  Alchemist, Healer and Mechanic in the skills list. Details on the inventory page is drawn like the other hints.
- **Icons for the weapon skills and Shield** in the skills lists (Axe, Bow, Pike, Sword, Thrown, Shield).
- **The minimap** sits at the screen's right edge with the rest of the HUD, its rooms redrawn crisply; the menus'
  panels are repainted, and the lists' scroll arrows are crisp arrows in the hints' ink.
- **Save and Load screens**: the saves keep their full-size pictures; the titles are redrawn.
- **Loot no longer flickers** half see-through as you walk up to it.
- **Emulator saves**: a Mupen64Plus `.mpk` (all four controllers' paks in one file; RMG and simple64 too) or a
  RetroArch `.srm` copied in as `aidyn.mpk` now works: the game takes the pak with an Aidyn save from it and keeps
  the whole original in `backups/`, also when it is put in while the game runs. Project64's and ares' paks worked
  already.
- **The manual (F1)** lists what Aidyn Plus changes, read from your own Aidyn Plus ROM: its spells, weapons, armour
  and shields, other items, every creature's level, weaknesses and resistances, training costs and potions.
- **Combat log in 4:3** steps out of the way while the held X or Y menu is open.
- **More room to draw**: a forest frame with the wider picture, the far map cells and the HUD already filled three
  quarters of the game's display list, and a busier one would have stopped the game ("GLIST OVERWRITE"). The lists are
  twice as big now, and the log notes any frame that comes close.
- **Fixes**: a model whose texture was damaged in memory is now skipped for a frame instead of stopping the game; the
  combat controls card no longer shows under the Train screen; the inventory page's list titles sit between their
  rule lines.
- **Release notes in every download**: `RELEASE-NOTES.md`, next to the README, has what each release changed, newest
  first.

**Known issue:** a save carried over from the original or Aidyn Patch into Aidyn Plus can crash it while loading
(their databases differ: #31). Keep each version's saves to itself.

Already installed on Linux? Unpack this release and run `bash install.sh` again: it replaces the game and keeps your
saves, settings and ROMs. On Windows, unpack it over the old folder or into a new one: your saves and settings are in
`%APPDATA%`, not in the game's folder.
