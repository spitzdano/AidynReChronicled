**Working with MDoulos.** MDoulos, author of the Action Edition mod, is working on a project of his own built on a new
engine. The two stay separate and share art, fixes and findings. Aidyn ReChronicled remains the original game and its
mods, fixed and improved.

## What's new since 0.9.0

- **Better shops** (Fair play, at New Game; the Original only): the shops carry Action Edition's stock, fuller and
  fitting each trade, in place of the original's thin shelves, with MDoulos's agreement. Off by default; **Old Stock**
  is the achievement for finishing the game with it off. Aidyn Patch, Aidyn Plus and Action Edition keep their own
  shops.
- **Combat descriptions are their own option.** What the highlighted spell, skill or item does, shown beside combat's
  menus, used to go away with the combat log. **Combat descriptions** on the ReChronicled page now turns them on or off
  by themselves (on by default).
- **The combat log keeps its corner on 16:9 screens.** With the HUD at the screen edges, the message box's portrait and
  text move right just enough for the log to fit in the bottom left corner, so it no longer hides while you hold X or Y.
  The dark band still runs across the whole screen. The combat HUD now also uses the window's real shape (with the
  aspect set to the window it assumed an ultrawide one).
- **Smaller button prompts** all round (85%), with their buttons drawn whole.
- **Menus fixed:** the Fair Play and Controls titles sit in their bars, the Controls rows below it, the Load Game title
  inside its bar, and the name entry's last row of letters inside its panel.
- **Shops and trainers** no longer show the conversation's last line under the page's torn bottom edge.
- **Fast-forward, explained:** in combat it doesn't add to Combat speed or Enemy turns; whichever is faster is used. The
  Help book and the README now say so.
- **Linux: an after-run hook.** `~/.config/AidynReChronicled/after-run`, if you make one, runs after each game (for
  example to copy your saves somewhere). Nothing changes without it.
