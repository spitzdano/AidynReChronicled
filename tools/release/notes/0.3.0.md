## What's new since 0.2.1

- **Save pictures in full resolution**: the Load and Save screens show each save's picture taken from the real
  rendered frame instead of the game's 64x32 smudge. The pictures go into `save-pictures/` beside your saves; saves
  made before this keep the small one until you save over them.
- **Button hints in the menus**: the treasure screen (Take / Put back, Leave, Switch), shops (Buy / Sell, Appraise,
  Switch), the inventory page (Equip, Unequip or Use for the highlighted item, Lists, and the party buttons when
  there's more than one of you), training after a battle (Train, Lists), and the combat menus held open with Y and X
  (Choose, Release to use).
- **Monsters, people and items from farther away**: they appear and move as far out as trees and rocks are drawn
  (80 units at the default `draw_distance` 2, 120 at 3; the game's own reach was 20), fading in at the edge.
- **No more footsteps from across the map**: enemies' footsteps and other animation sounds fade out with distance
  again instead of wrapping around to loud past 15 units.
- **Text fix**: text no longer comes out squeezed ("LCAD GAME") when an older HD menu pack is installed; the new
  letter widths are used only with a pack built for them.
- **Steam shortcut**: `steam_shortcut.py` takes `--launcher`, `--art` and `--icon` for a shortcut to another launcher.
