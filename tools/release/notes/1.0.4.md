**Aidyn ReChronicled 1.0.4.** Through **Updates** on the title menu. There's no new download: a new install takes 1.0.0 and updates from there.

## New

- **The pause menu's lists are redrawn.** Inventory, Spells and Character now have tabs (LB / RB: the kinds of item, the schools of magic), a sort (Y: as the game keeps it, by type, by name), headings, a scroll bar and the mouse (point at a row, click a tab). They are drawn in the style of the page, and the page can be turned with the **right stick** instead of LB / RB.
- **Details (X).** Switch it on and the left half of the page shows what the highlighted item, spell or skill is and does, in place of the paper doll. For an item it compares its numbers with what your party member wears now, says what it would replace, and keeps a row of what they wear underneath. It stays on as you move between items, lists and characters; a new game starts with it off. With a mouse, pointing at a row shows its card for a moment even with Details off.
- **A Character page.** The Stats page is folded into the skills: the six attributes sit above the skills, with what your equipment adds or takes away, and the experience to your next level.
- **Train a stat any time.** On the Character page, press Up from the first skill to move onto the attributes, choose one with left and right, press A to see the next point's cost and A again to raise it, with the experience you have unspent, as the screen after a fight does (same cost, same rules). It works with the mouse too.

## Changed

- **Descriptions for more items.** Scrolls, amulets, ingredients and the other things the game describes now have a card.
- **LB / RB no longer turn the pause menu's pages.** They are the tabs; the right stick turns pages. The keyboard's camera keys do the same.
- **The Menus help topic and the F1 manual describe the new pages.**

## Fixed

- **The pause menu was slow with 8x anti-aliasing and supersampling together**. Anti-aliasing now stops at 4x while supersampling is on; your setting is kept.

## Settings

The lists and Details have three settings in `settings.json` (`graphics`): `tabbed_lists` (`false`: the game's own lists and Stats page), `inventory_sort` (0, 1 or 2) and `details`.
