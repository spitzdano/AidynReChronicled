## What's new since 0.1.0

- **Sound effects in combat and while walking**: footsteps, swings, hits and spells were silent; they play again.
- **Shorter start**: the title music now starts where it used to when the title screen appeared, instead of
  playing through the skipped logo videos.
- **Fast-forward**: left stick click (or hold Tab) runs the game at 3x; enemy turns in combat run at 2x on their own.
- **Button prompts**: a controls card while walking and in combat (View shows / hides it); LB / RB shown beside the
  pause pages' arrows.
- **Newly painted art**: every portrait, the item and slot icons, the button prompts, and the first world textures
  (Alaron, the starting forest, part of Gwernia Castle); F2 switches it off and on to compare. Redrawn HUD pieces:
  page arrows and a rope HitPoints bar.
- **Combat HUD at the screen edges** in widescreen; the party portraits stay centred.
- **Camera**: up / down turns like left / right, right stick click recentres; `camera_invert_y` / `camera_invert_x`.
- **Save backups**: the last 20 states of your saves in `backups/`.
