## What to try in this test build (0.8.2-beta.1)

This is a patch over 0.8.1 (README.txt in the download says how to put it over). It is a test of keyboard and mouse play
and of two fixes; the key pictures and some wording are still plain placeholders. Anything odd, or anything you'd change:
the Problem report form (https://github.com/spitzdano/AidynReChronicled/issues/new?template=problem-report.yml; say it's
the test build).

- **Keyboard and mouse.** W A S D (or the arrows) move; Z confirms (A) and X goes back (B); the mouse turns and tilts the
  camera while you walk (its middle button puts the camera behind the party again); the left and right mouse buttons are
  A and B. Does it feel right? Is the camera too fast or too slow (Mouse sensitivity in Controls)?
- **Controls** (the title screen's menu, or the pause menu: ReChronicled, then Controls near the bottom): every key and
  mouse button can be changed, two to an action. Press A on a row, then the key, mouse button or wheel step you want;
  Esc cancels, Y clears it. Please try rebinding a few, and Reset to defaults.
- **Conversations say how to move on:** "Continue Z" (or the A button's picture with a pad) at the bottom right.
- **The controls card (LB while walking) shows your keys** beside each line while the keyboard or mouse is what you used
  last; the Help book's keyboard page (Menu, Help, ReChronicled) lists the current ones.
- **The Help book's ReChronicled topic is back.** It had dropped out of 0.8.1 (one command too many).

Not in this build: key and mouse pictures instead of text, and achievements.
