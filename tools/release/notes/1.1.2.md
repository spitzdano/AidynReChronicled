**Aidyn ReChronicled 1.1.2.** Through **Updates** on the title menu on Linux and Windows. macOS has a new download for this version: a new Mac install, or an update from the release page, takes it from here.

## New

- **Fights stay won can be turned off in a game under way.** If you would rather fight a won group again (to level), the ReChronicled page has a **Fights stay won** row: press A twice to turn it off for the rest of that game. The Settled Scores achievement is given up for that game.
- **macOS: a ROMs folder.** The game makes `~/Library/Application Support/AidynReChronicled/ROMs` every time it starts. Put your ROMs in it (the 1.0 release, Aidyn Patch, Aidyn Plus, Action Edition: any of them, under any name) and the game finds them by what is in them, so each shows in the title screen's version list (Y). In Finder, Go > Go to Folder and paste the path.
- **A ROM the game does not accept is named.** A file in the ROMs folder that is Aidyn Chronicles but none of the four ROMs (another release, an older Action Edition, a patch made from a different ROM) used to be skipped without a word. Now the log names it, and the version list says so under a version that is missing.

## Changed

- **The I key can be told from a 1.** The cap for I, which opens the version list on the title screen, now has serifs.
- **One cap for each key on the D-pad and right-stick prompts.** Switch and Lists show their left and right keys, Scroll and Choose their up and down keys, and the right stick's pages and a shop's Buy | Sell their two camera keys, each on its own cap, where they were run together on one (A D, or two brackets that read as an I). Scroll and Choose had shown the left and right keys. A shop's Buy | Sell caps are larger.

## Fixed

- **A trainer's prices follow the character you are looking at.** The list and the card priced the first member of the party whoever was shown, so Warrior could read 16000 experience for a character who needs 1000. A skill a character cannot learn shows a dash, and **Can't learn** on its card, where it showed 0.
- **A stack of items shows what is left.** Equipping one of "2 Dagger" left "2 Dagger" in the list; it now shows the one left.
