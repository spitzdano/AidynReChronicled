**Aidyn ReChronicled 1.1.0.** Through **Updates** on the title menu. There's no new download: a new install takes 1.0.0 and updates from there.

## New

- **The Bestiary.** On the map page, under Journal: a page of the creatures you have met, each with Alaron's sketch and a note on it. A creature is entered the first time it stands in one of your fights.
- **What the Bestiary tells grows with your fights.** A rough pencil sketch after one fight, an ink drawing with its level and hit points after 3, the finished colour drawing after 5, its resistances and weapons after 6, its attributes after 10. A creature fought only once in the story shows everything at once.
- **Sense Aura counts too.** Rank 3 shows level and hit points, rank 7 resistances and weapons, rank 9 the attributes, whichever you reach first. A page that is still short says what the next step needs. The book is kept with each save and the other versions' spellings count as one creature.
- **Your party shows how hurt they are.** Every party member has four damage portraits, painted for each of them, that show as their hit points fall below 80, 60, 40 and 20 per cent: in the pause menu, the party bar, the combat columns and the turn strip, and on the Load and Save Game slots as the game was saved. The new **Damaged portraits** row on the ReChronicled page turns it off.
- **A new Alaron.** A painted portrait to match the rest of the party.
- **The pause menu's lists are redrawn.** Inventory, Spells and Character now have tabs (LB / RB: the kinds of item, the schools of magic), a sort (Y: as the game keeps it, by type, by name), headings, a scroll bar and the mouse (point at a row, click a tab). They are drawn in the style of the page, and the page can be turned with the **right stick** instead of LB / RB.
- **Details (X).** Switch it on and the left half of the page shows what the highlighted item, spell or skill is and does, in place of the paper doll. For an item it compares its numbers with what your party member wears now, says what it would replace, and keeps a row of what they wear underneath. It stays on as you move between items, lists and characters; a new game starts with it off. With a mouse, pointing at a row shows its card for a moment even with Details off.
- **A Character page.** The Stats page is folded into the skills: the six attributes sit above the skills, with what your equipment adds or takes away, and the experience to your next level.
- **Train a stat any time.** On the Character page, press Up from the first skill to move onto the attributes, choose one with left and right, press A to see the next point's cost and A again to raise it, with the experience you have unspent, as the screen after a fight does (same cost, same rules). It works with the mouse too.
- **After a fight.** The Train screen's pages are the new Attributes, Character and Spells, with the cost of what you pick shown as you move.
- **The loot screen.** Your side of a chest is the same tabbed list, with Take and Drop shown beside Leave and Switch.
- **Shops and trainers.** A shop is one list with **Buy** and **Sell** tabs (LB / RB), prices in the list, and the card for the highlighted item at the left with its price, or what the keeper pays, and how it compares with what your party member wears. A trainer is the same: **Spells** and **Skills** tabs, what each costs in experience in the list, and the card with the experience, gold and what you have to spend. A still buys, sells and trains; Y appraises; B leaves.
- **Skills you can use outside a fight** (Alchemist, Healer, Mechanic) say **(Use)** on the Character page.
- **Pop-ups in the style of the page.** How many to sell, are you sure, and the Save and Load pages' questions are drawn on parchment, with the chosen answer marked.

## Changed

- **Descriptions for more items.** Scrolls, amulets, ingredients and the other things the game describes now have a card.
- **Smoother outlines in the world.** The painted cut-outs (signs, fences, banners, torches and the like) have rounded outlines in place of the N64's stepped edge.
- **Painted potions and scrolls.** A bottle for every potion and a scroll for each school of magic, painted by MDoulos.
- **Alaron's Battle Axe** has a brushed steel head.
- **A click is A only on a row** in these lists (a click on a portrait or beside the scroll bar did whatever A did to the highlighted item).
- **The titles Inventory, Spells and Character change with the list** instead of sliding in after it.
- **LB / RB no longer turn the pause menu's pages.** They are the tabs; the right stick turns pages. The keyboard's camera keys do the same.
- **The Menus help topic and the F1 manual describe the new pages.**

## Fixed

- **A Controller Pak save lost its Bestiary.** Loading a game from a pause-menu slot started with an empty book; the book (and the Fair play rules) now come back. Games saved before this are found too.
- **The pause menu showed the game's own list for a moment as it closed.** The tabbed list stays until the page is gone.
- **The pause menu was slow with 8x anti-aliasing and supersampling together**. Anti-aliasing now stops at 4x while supersampling is on; your setting is kept.

## Settings

The ReChronicled page has two new rows: **Tabbed lists** (the lists and Stats page described above, or the game's own) and **Damaged portraits**. In `settings.json` (`graphics`): `tabbed_lists`, `damaged_portraits`, `inventory_sort` (0, 1 or 2) and `details`.
