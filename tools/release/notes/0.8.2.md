## What's new since 0.8.1

- **Keyboard and mouse play (#44).** W A S D (or the arrows) move, the mouse turns and tilts the camera while you walk,
  and the left and right mouse buttons are A and B. **Controls** (the title screen's menu, or the pause menu's
  ReChronicled page) changes any key or mouse button, two to an action, and the mouse's sensitivity.
- **The prompts show your keys.** While the keyboard or mouse is what you used last, every prompt (the controls card,
  the menu hints, the page arrows, the Help book, conversations) shows the key or mouse button you press, painted in the
  style of the turn order bar; touch the controller and its buttons come back.
- **Conversations say how to move on (#43):** "Continue" with the button or key at the bottom right, so keyboard
  players aren't stuck at the first conversation; now also when you talk to someone in the field (a shopkeeper).
- **Start skips a conversation,** as it skips the opening and the other cinematics: the lines go by on their own until
  it asks you something or ends, and everything it does still happens. Start again stops it.
- **Action Edition (#45):** the community Action Edition mod (faster combat, a rebalanced economy and items, shops
  stocked to their themes) plays as a fourth version next to the original, Aidyn Patch and Aidyn Plus. Bring your own
  ROM of it (`AidynActionFeb072026.z64`) and put it in the `ROMs` folder as it is.
- **A Versions list on the title screen.** Y (keyboard I) opens it: Original, then the mods. Choose one with A and the
  game restarts into it. The bottom left names the version you're playing.
- **Achievements (#42):** 70 milestones (the story, optional side quests and bosses, companions, exploration, growth,
  feats and fair play), each with its medal in the metal of the version you play (bronze, silver, gold or rose
  copper). The story, side quests, companions and optional bosses stay `???` until you earn them; the rest show from
  the start, and every one you can see says how it is earned. A toast shows each one as it happens; the title menu's
  **Achievements** shows the progress of all four versions (Original, Aidyn Patch, Aidyn Plus, Action Edition), and
  **Achievements** on the pause menu's Menu page lists this version's with the day earned. Only what you do in play
  counts: a save you load never unlocks what it already holds.
- **Fair play:** a New Game offers fixes for the original's loopholes, each on unless you turn it off: healers can't
  heal each other up for free (Honest Healer), shops can't be bought from and sold back to at a profit (Fair Trader),
  brewed potions sell for no more than their ingredients (True Alchemist), and chests you empty stay empty (Fair
  Finder; a few used to refill, two with thousands of gold), each with an achievement for finishing the game with it
  on, and monsters you beat stay beaten (the few groups that came back, like the inn cellar's bats, don't). Action
  Edition's shops are its own fix, so Fair trade isn't offered there. The cheat codes now stop achievements too.
- **Treasure sparkle:** a star over the chests and hidden treasure near you that you can see, until you open them. Off
  unless you choose it at New Game or on the ReChronicled page; once on it stays on for that game, and Keen Eye is for
  finishing without it.
- **The party's portraits in the field** sit in a bronze bar like the combat turn order's (with the redrawn art).
- **The Versions list names who made each version** (the mods' authors: halibabica, MDoulos).
- **Sholeh's intelligence no longer drops to 3 after loading a save.**
- **The ReChronicled page scrolls**, ten rows at a time with room between them, a bar at the right showing where you are.
- **END, STA and VIG close with the pause menu** instead of staying on the screen as the scroll rolls up.
- **Restored sound effects (#34):** the effects' samples went through the same restoration as the music's (the
  Instruments row on the ReChronicled page switches both).
- **A crash after changing the draw distance is fixed (#27).** In a big forest at a long draw distance more than 127
  trees share one picture, which the game's count couldn't hold: a picture was freed while trees were still drawn
  with it ("Skipped a damaged texture", then a crash). The count now goes past 127 safely.
- **The Help book's ReChronicled topic is back (#46).** It had dropped out of 0.8.1 (one command too many).
- **The Load Game page** has room for its picture and its buttons.

Already installed on Linux? Unpack this release and run `bash install.sh` again: it replaces the game and keeps your
saves, settings and ROMs. On Windows, unpack it over the old folder or into a new one: your saves and settings are in
`%APPDATA%`, not in the game's folder.
