## What's new since 0.8.1

- **Keyboard and mouse play (#44).** W A S D (or the arrows) move, the mouse turns and tilts the camera while you walk,
  and the left and right mouse buttons are A and B. **Controls** (the title screen's menu, or the pause menu's
  ReChronicled page) changes any key or mouse button, two to an action, and the mouse's sensitivity.
- **The prompts show your keys.** While the keyboard or mouse is what you used last, every prompt (the controls card,
  the menu hints, the page arrows, the Help book, conversations) shows the key or mouse button you press, painted in the
  style of the turn order bar; touch the controller and its buttons come back.
- **Conversations say how to move on (#43):** "Continue" with the button or key at the bottom right, so keyboard
  players aren't stuck at the first conversation.
- **Action Edition (#45):** the community Action Edition mod (faster combat, a rebalanced economy and items, shops
  stocked to their themes) plays as a fourth version next to the original, Aidyn Patch and Aidyn Plus. Bring your own
  ROM of it (`AidynActionFeb072026.z64`) and put it in the `ROMs` folder as it is.
- **A Versions list on the title screen.** Y (keyboard I) opens it: Original, then the mods. Choose one with A and the
  game restarts into it. The bottom left names the version you're playing.
- **Achievements (#42):** 61 milestones (the story, optional side quests and bosses, companions, exploration, growth
  and feats), each hidden until you earn it, its medal in the metal of the version you play (bronze, silver, gold or
  rose copper). A toast shows each one as it happens; the title menu's **Achievements**
  shows the progress of all four versions (Original, Aidyn Patch, Aidyn Plus, Action Edition), and the pause menu's
  ReChronicled page lists this version's with the day earned. Only what you do in play counts: a save you load
  never unlocks what it already holds.
- **Restored sound effects (#34):** the effects' samples went through the same restoration as the music's (the
  Instruments row on the ReChronicled page switches both).
- **A crash after changing the draw distance is fixed (#27).** In a big forest at a long draw distance more than 127
  trees share one picture, which the game's count couldn't hold: a picture was freed while trees were still drawn
  with it ("Skipped a damaged texture", then a crash). The count now goes past 127 safely.
- **The Help book's ReChronicled topic is back (#46).** It had dropped out of 0.8.1 (one command too many).
- **The Load Game page** has room for its picture and its buttons.

Already installed on Linux? Unpack this release and run `bash install.sh` again: it replaces the game and keeps your
saves, settings and ROMs. On Windows, unpack it over the old folder or into a new one: your saves and settings are in
`%APPDATA%`, not in the game's folder.
