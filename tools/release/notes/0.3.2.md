## What's new since 0.3.1

- **Menu lettering with any HD menu pack**: the inventory page's HitPoints, Level, Gold and DETAILS, and the page
  titles (Treasure, Inventory, Buy, Sell, Skills, Spells, Stats, Train) and the map's town names, are in the text font
  whatever version of `hd-ui` is installed, or none. The titles come with the release (`rechronicled-text`); the words
  painted into the inventory page are redrawn by the game itself over its own page picture, since the page can't
  ship. An older `hd-ui` used to show the old lettering.
- **Monsters, people and items twice as far**: with the default `draw_distance` 2 they're drawn out to 160 units
  (was 80; the game's own was 20 and 5-30), so a chest or a monster in view no longer vanishes as you back away.
  Opened and blown-open chests and bags reach as far too. Footsteps and other
  animation sounds still fade out at 15 units.
- **No more light blue seams**: thin bright lines where pieces of the world meet (across the ground, up wall and cliff
  corners) are filled in by the renderer. Below the horizon the background also fades to a dark shade, so holes near
  the camera are dark instead of bright blue.
- **No flicker when crossing into the next part of the map**: walking over a map-cell boundary showed one frame
  slightly out of place at high refresh rates; that frame is now shown without blending.
- **489 more painted textures**: characters (247), items (89), spell and other effects (31) and more (122), in the
  hand-painted style of the first batch.
