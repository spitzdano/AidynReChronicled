**Aidyn ReChronicled 1.0.2.** Through **Updates** on the title menu. There's no new download: a new install takes 1.0.0 and updates from there.

## New

- **Resolution on the Display page:** Auto, 720, 1080 (the default), 1440 or 2160. Auto draws at your window's own height, up to 4K (2160 lines): a 1440-line screen gets 1440, a 1080-line one 1080, and it follows the window across monitors and fullscreen. Supersampling now works with Auto too (the window's height times 2x), so on a 4K screen leave it Off. Set it before starting a game from **Display** on the title menu, or in the pause menu.
