## What's new since 0.3.2

- **Aidyn Plus**: halibabica's bigger rebalance (new weapon skills and items, every spell learnable, elements and
  resistances reworked) can be played as a third version, next to the original and Aidyn Patch. Like Aidyn Patch it
  isn't in the download: apply `AidynPlus.bps` to your own 1.0 ROM and give the game the result once (`--rom`, or the
  "Aidyn ReChronicled (Aidyn Plus)" launcher asks for it). It is checked by its hash (SHA-1
  `d57f9459a77c66490ff09b3d9d97545c7a65fad2`) and keeps its own saves.
- **Version list on the title screen**: the bottom left lists Original, Aidyn Patch and Aidyn Plus, the one you're
  playing lit. X and Y switch to the other two, as marked beside them (View no longer switches). One you haven't added
  is grey and marked *not found*. From the title menu (New Game, Load Game, Options), B now goes back to the title.
- **Turn order in combat**: a strip across the top shows the round's turns from the fighter whose turn it is, a team
  colour under each portrait, then the next round, dimmed. The next round is shown exactly as it will go. Quick
  fighters can act two or three times a round (from DEX), and appear that many times. A crowded round gets smaller
  portraits and a "+N". `turn_order` in `settings.json` turns it off.
- **Targets flash**: whoever you're targeting pulses on their portrait and in the turn order, along with the game's own
  pulse on their model.
- **Easier aiming with a bow or an area spell**: the aiming marker moves at about the N64's pace (it was three times
  faster and skipped past enemies) and follows how far you push the stick, so a light push aims finely. With a bow,
  left / right on the D-pad jump to the previous / next target.
- **Fewer holes in the ground**: below the horizon, wider gaps are filled too (up to about four N64 pixels), such as a
  wall that doesn't quite reach the ground; thin cracks with soft edges no longer slip through.
- **Distant scenery stays put**: parts of the far landscape (the castle across the bay in the title flyover, hills and
  shores) no longer vanish as you move from one part of the map to the next, and the map cells the game left out
  are drawn too. Scenery at the picture's edges no longer pops in when the camera tilts, and a new area's far
  landscape fills in faster.
